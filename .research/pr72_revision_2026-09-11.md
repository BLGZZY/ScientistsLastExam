## What the first-draw evidence actually establishes

`.research/analyze_pr72_first_draws.py` recomputes the decomposition from the
two published, source-matched reviewer records. It makes no evaluator or model
calls and retains all scheduled first proposals. The resulting JSON binds the
input bytes and candidate hashes.

The strongest first proposal and reference use 12 units on every development
world and have identical correct discrete decisions. Its development advantage
0.0369028226 therefore comes entirely from continuous mass utility: the mean
supported-world utility increases by 0.04428338712. On heldout, one additional
correct outside-family refusal explains 1/24 of the 0.0953014357 score increase;
the remainder corresponds to +0.06436172284 mean supported-world utility.

This narrows the next investigation to mass estimation and joint residual
rejection. It does not reveal the candidate's algorithm or its target allocation.
In particular, aggregate data cannot justify asserting that adaptive acquisition,
more optimizer iterations, a particular halo prior, or a grid search caused the
gain. The private candidate source is required to establish that.

The timed-out proposal remains invalid despite its development score. The weaker
valid proposal is not substituted for the stronger one to pass admission.

## Reference allocation revision

The shared-grid construction check exposed inadequate mass recovery by the
historical equal-allocation reference: a local diagnostic gave development
0.5242163444 and heldout 0.4240799883, failing the unchanged 60% constant-mass
bound on heldout. Both splits are therefore construction-exposed, not fresh
confirmation. The failed method is retained as `reference_equal_allocation.py`
and included in the frozen Linux replay, rather than discarding its result.

The revised standard witness uses a one-unit-per-target pilot, fits both laws,
then greedily allocates nine additional units to minimize estimated log-mass
variance after profiling nuisance directions in the expected Poisson Fisher
information. Both pilot law fits enter with relative-likelihood weights. The
reference then jointly fits all paid counts. There are no seed-specific branches,
new hidden priors or threshold changes. The equal-allocation and three previous
ability removals are declared before measuring this method. Independent tests
check expected counts against the oracle, noiseless fitted mass and exact budget
accounting. A noiseless expected-count fixture can still legitimately abstain
when the finite exposure cannot statistically distinguish two laws.

This is a constructor response to an exposed weakness, not independent validation
or a resolution of D16. Only a new frozen, independent first-draw campaign can
address the latter.
